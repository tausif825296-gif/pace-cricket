About Us

A Place Where Cricket Dreams Begin

Pace Cricket Academy Ranchi is a professional cricket training centre at Hi.q International Academy, Bariatu. We focus on technique, fitness, match awareness and mental strength to build confident cricketers.

Every player gets personal attention, structured drills and regular match practice. Whether you are picking up a bat for the first time or preparing for district and state trials, our programs are designed for you.
